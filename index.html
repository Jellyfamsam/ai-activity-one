💡 How Preference Matching Works

  • Preferred Time Windows (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) highlight matching slots with a gold ★ Match badge.
  • Preferred Barber selection automatically locks the chair to that stylist's specific open blocks. Choosing "First Available" searches all active chairs for maximum flexibility.
  • Atmosphere tags (Silent chair, sensitive skin) are printed directly on the barber's roster so your chair is prepped prior to your arrival.
